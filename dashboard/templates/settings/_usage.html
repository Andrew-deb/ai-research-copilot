{# =========================================================================== Usage — what this account has done. Its own section rather than a block at the foot of Account: it is the one part of settings that is a dataset rather than a set of controls, and it carries tabs of its own. Two tabs, answering two different questions. Today how much of your allowance is left. Server-rendered, because it is one cheap lookup and it is what most people came for. Analytics what your use looks like over a week or a month. Fetched when the tab is opened — two aggregate queries against a database several hundred milliseconds away, for a view most visits never open. Nothing here is editable. The allowances are operator configuration, and a settings page that let somebody raise their own ceiling would not be a settings page. =========================================================================== #}
