Help & docs

Short answers to the questions that come up first.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. You can browse the corpus, open any paper, run semantic search and ask a limited number of questions without signing in. An account is what lets you keep things: collections, notes and reading progress.

Keyword search or semantic search?

Keyword search matches the words you typed. Semantic search matches meaning, so "how do models avoid making things up" finds papers about hallucination and grounding without those words appearing in your query. It is the better default for a question; keyword is better for a known title.

What does the percentage next to a result mean?

Cosine similarity between your question and the best-matching passage in that paper. Higher is closer. A badge reading from the conclusion means the match came from that section rather than the abstract.

Why do some papers have no sections?

Because no open-access copy could be read — there is no free copy, the publisher declines automated access, or the PDF is a scan with no text layer. Those papers stay searchable by abstract.

What are the daily limits for?

Search and answers cost real compute. The limits keep the demo available for everyone rather than letting one visitor exhaust it — see plans.
