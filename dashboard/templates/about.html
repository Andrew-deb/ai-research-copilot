About Research Copilot

A research workspace with an assistant at the centre, rather than a search engine with a chatbot attached.

What it does

It builds a focused corpus around the topics you care about, reads the papers it can reach, and answers questions with citations back to the section the claim came from — not just the title.

How papers get here

Papers are discovered through OpenAlex, filtered to the research fields you choose, then scored for relevance against your topics before anything is indexed. Where an open-access copy exists, the Conclusion, Discussion and Methods sections are extracted and indexed alongside the abstract, so a question about findings can be answered from findings.

What it will not do

It does not paywall-bypass. Where only a publisher copy exists and that publisher declines automated access, the paper stays in the index with its abstract and is honest about the rest.

It does not invent citations. Answers are drawn from the indexed corpus, and when the corpus cannot answer a question the assistant says so.

Built on

Databricks Lakebase with pgvector for retrieval, a Spark pipeline for ingestion, and an MCP server exposing the research tools to the assistant.
